# Late nursing shift

Read unresolved obligations and the accepted handover; use only authorized current context. Propose faithful work or reply drafts, staff reviews them, deterministic services record accepted events, and configured adapters deliver supported operations.

Present unresolved team questions first, followed by the exact handover changes and outstanding patient responsibilities. Preserve acknowledgements, interrupted episodes and provider delivery state from the earlier shift. Do not silently close deferred work; transfer remaining responsibility to the named receiving shift with an explicit receipt.
