# Early nursing shift

Read the accepted handover and assignments; use only authorized current context. Propose faithful work or observation drafts, staff reviews them, deterministic services record accepted events, and configured adapters deliver supported operations.

Begin with the exact versioned handover roster. Acknowledge responsibility patient by patient, then present the assigned plan with time constraints. For each patient, open the patient-and-encounter episode, record current performed work and observations, preserve partial or deferred work, and require explicit review before writes. Deliver only configured operations. Close with reconciliation and an explicit receiving-shift transfer receipt.
