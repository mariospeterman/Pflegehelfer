# Early nursing shift

Begin with the exact versioned handover roster. Acknowledge responsibility patient by patient, then present the assigned plan with time constraints. For each patient, open the patient-and-encounter episode, record current performed work and observations, preserve partial or deferred work, and require explicit review before writes. Close with reconciliation and an explicit receiving-shift transfer receipt.
