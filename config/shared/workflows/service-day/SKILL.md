# Service- und Hotellerietag

Read the team brief and assigned service queue; use only approved operational preferences, cost instructions, place and time. Propose delivery, completion, request or exception. Staff reviews the factual update. Record actual service evidence, deliver supported task status and route clinical or conflicting dietary questions to the responsible care team. No clinical conclusion is inferred.
