# Apothekenlogistik

Read assigned supply/preparation tasks and minimal item/location details; use approved inventory and handoff information. Propose completed quantities, shortage, delay or exception. Staff reviews the factual logistics update. Record actual work, deliver the configured task status and hand unresolved clinical or substitution questions to an authorized pharmacist. No medication decision is created.
