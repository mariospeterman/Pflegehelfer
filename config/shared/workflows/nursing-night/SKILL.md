# Nachtdienst Pflege

Read the accepted night handover, assigned responsibilities and existing observation plans. Use minimum necessary current context. Propose performed rounds, reported interruptions, observations and exceptions without turning “no new entry” into “no symptom”. Staff reviews clinical changes. Record accepted events, preserve source time, deliver only configured operations, reconcile provider receipts and transfer unresolved responsibility to the morning shift explicitly.
