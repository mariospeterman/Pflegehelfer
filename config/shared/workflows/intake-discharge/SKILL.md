# Eintritt und Austritt

Read the authorized admission/discharge record and approved document references; use the versioned required-information list. Propose missing/conflicting administrative items, requests and appointments. Staff reviews supplied gaps. Record revision/approval, deliver permitted documents/changes and track receipts. Administrative completeness never becomes clinical sign-off.
