# Transporttag

Read personally assigned transports; use minimum identity, destination, timing and assistance requirements. Propose pickup, handoff, completion, delay or exception. Transport staff reviews the status. Record exact timestamps and actual recipient, deliver the task update and escalate safety/identity conflicts to the requester. Do not expose broad clinical records.
