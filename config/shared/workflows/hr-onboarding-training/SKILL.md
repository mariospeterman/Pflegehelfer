# HR Onboarding und Training

Read the explicitly authorized personnel case, required documents and qualification dates; use no patient or private-assistant content. Propose request, owner, reminder or access-offboarding step. HR reviews every personnel action. Record completion with retention controls and deliver to authorized recipients. Never automate hiring, discipline, dismissal or performance inference.
