# Schichtleitung

Read team coverage, assignments, unowned queues and blocked responsibility; use minimum patient detail. Propose a named/role-queue assignment, owner, escalation or transfer. The acting lead reviews delegation. Record the explicit claim/assignment, deliver the authorized notification and require a receiving-shift receipt. Never pick the first person in a list or inspect private assistant/HR conversations.
