# Finanzreview

Read authorized aggregates, accepted service-evidence status and export receipts; use the approved period/rule version. Propose reconciliation item, exception owner or review. Finance staff verifies the evidence. Record reviewed outcomes and deliver only approved exports. Do not infer tariffs, create revenue forecasts from clinical text or expose individual clinical details.
