## Current assessment

The running result is the intended conversational clinical coworker, not the retired dashboard. A staff member can receive the six-patient handover, explicitly choose context, work through planned or alarm/spontaneous episodes, document several facts naturally, revise and restore one review, reuse approved documentation in the active episode, communicate with the team, defer/resume responsibility, transfer to the next shift and distinguish local acceptance, Medplum storage and provider confirmation. Better models improve language and presentation but cannot replace policy, typed execution, Medplum, adapters, approvals or audit.

It is **not production-ready for real patient data or clinical use**. Remaining internal P1 work includes: migrate direct mutation/workday coupling to atomic acceptance; complete workflow/read-set binding; resource-native Medplum reconstruction; append-only handover addenda; relational inbound/conflicts and multi-replica replay; governed pack publication; ACL-enforced shared threads; RLS/least privilege, retention, two-replica and real restore proof; and formal clinical/model evaluation. External gates are institutional identity/device proof, private vendor contracts, accepted Swiss-German model/audio artifacts, deployment approvals and production backup/restore evidence.
