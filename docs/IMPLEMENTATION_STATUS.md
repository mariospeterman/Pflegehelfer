## Current assessment

This is now the intended GenUI-first synthetic product rather than the retired dashboard. The UI, transport and first operational workflow/session slice are materially integrated and testable. It is **not production-ready for real patient data** because the intent/command transaction, provider workers, durable SSE replay and removal of the clinical checkpoint path are still internally incomplete, while identity/vendor/model/profile/environment approvals remain external gates.

The repository must not claim 100% production completion until every internal `PARTIAL` above is closed and the clean-checkout audit passes in a production-equivalent environment.
