## Current assessment

The running result is the intended conversational clinical coworker, not the retired dashboard. A staff member can receive the six-patient handover, explicitly choose context, work through planned or alarm/spontaneous episodes, document several facts naturally, review only the proposed changes, communicate with the team, defer/resume responsibility, transfer to the next shift and observe Medplum/provider synchronization. Better models improve language understanding and bounded presentation but cannot replace or bypass policy, typed execution, Medplum, provider adapters, approvals or audit.

It is **not production-ready for real patient data or clinical use**. Remaining internal P1 work is: one accepted-command transaction spanning operational mutation/authority/audit/FHIR/provider outbox; complete workflow-step/policy/proposal/read-set intent binding; immutable handover clinical-delta/resource-version evidence (the current hash freezes roster identity only); relational leased provider workers/inbound processing and durable replica-safe SSE; Medplum reconstruction without the checkpoint Binary; executable workflow-step progression/publishing; tenant RLS/least privilege, retention sweepers and multi-replica safety; and formal clinical/model evaluation. External gates are institutional OIDC/device identity, private vendor contracts, validated Swiss-German model/ASR artifacts, deployment security/privacy/clinical approvals and production backup/restore evidence.
