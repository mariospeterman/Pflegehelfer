## Current assessment

This is the intended conversational GenUI-first synthetic product rather than the retired dashboard. The demo now covers six-patient handover and planning, natural multi-fact documentation, explicit no-action language, corrections/uncertainty, patient interruption/resume, structured team-question answer/close controls, a semantic patient protocol/profile, bounded approval and simulated provider delivery. The original blue conversation/P mark with green activity signal avoids any red-cross/crescent emblem. A better LLM can add grounded meaning and presentation quality, but cannot grant permission or bypass deterministic action validation.

It is **not production-ready for real patient data or clinical use**. Durable multi-replica intent/voice/approval authority bound to organization/session/thread/workflow/context/proposal/policy, exact assignment-item responsibility and receiving-shift acceptance, one accepted-command boundary spanning operational state/FHIR/outbox, relational leased provider workers, tenant/RLS enforcement, site-scoped Medplum control resources and resource reconstruction without the checkpoint Binary are internal P1 blockers. Formal held-out clinical-language evaluation is also required. Real identity, private vendor contracts, institution policies, validated Swiss-German models and deployment approvals remain external gates.

Manual real-PWA review confirmed the 6-patient handover, patient switch, team answer/close, semantic patient profile, no overlap and no console-visible fatal state.

The repository must not claim 100% production completion until every internal `PARTIAL` above is closed and the clean-checkout audit passes in a production-equivalent environment.
