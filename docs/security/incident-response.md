# Incident response

1. Triage safety and confidentiality; preserve append-only audit and infrastructure evidence.
2. Revoke sessions/credentials, isolate affected adapters/nodes, disable AI or writes while retaining read-only core operation.
3. Notify the controller, security lead, clinical safety owner, DPO, and vendors under the approved matrix.
4. Determine patients, users, data, time window, and integrity impact without copying PHI into tickets.
5. Restore from verified artifacts/backups, reconcile authoritative systems, and validate clinical continuity.
6. Complete required notifications, root-cause review, control updates, and evidence retention.
