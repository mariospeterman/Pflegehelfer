# Retention model

Retention is policy-driven, not hard-coded. Clinical records follow the authoritative institution record schedule; workflow records are retained only as needed for care and accountability; provider payloads are removed after reconciliation; transient transcripts are deleted after approved structuring unless formally filed; device caches purge on logout, relationship loss, expiry, or remote wipe; security audit retention is set by the controller. Legal hold overrides deletion through an approved documented process.
