# Intended use

Pflegehelfer is administrative documentation and coordination middleware for
authorised staff in Swiss inpatient rehabilitation and long-term care. It
retrieves exact authorised records, preserves attributed staff/source meaning,
prepares human-reviewed documentation, coordinates already authorised work and
synchronises accepted records through explicit adapters. It does not decide
what care a person needs.

Excluded uses are patient-specific diagnosis, prognosis, physiological or
symptom interpretation, clinical risk ranking/triage, treatment/medication/dose
or urgent-intervention recommendations, new care-plan generation, device
control, primary alarm replacement and automated or recommended employment
decisions. Human review, a site configuration or a model response cannot enable
an excluded use.

The synthetic demo is for engineering and validation only. This intended-use
statement and the technical controls do not themselves determine legal
classification. A production deployment requires institution-approved
identity, policies, data processing, terminology, vendor contracts, security,
regulatory assessment and governance.
