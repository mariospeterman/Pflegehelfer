# DPIA working draft

Status: working engineering input, not legal approval. High-risk factors include health data, vulnerable persons, free text/voice, cross-system aggregation, role mistakes, offline devices, and optional AI. Controls implemented in the reference slice include data minimisation, managed-device and relationship checks, purpose enforcement, no-store responses, explicit approval, source/version visibility, audit chaining, fail-closed AI, and non-authoritative alarms.

Before production, the controller must document lawful basis, necessity/proportionality, data-subject handling, processors/transfers, residual risks, retention, incident process, DPIA approval, and consultation needs under the Swiss FADP and applicable cantonal/sector rules.
